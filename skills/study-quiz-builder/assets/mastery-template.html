Unit 1 Mastery Quiz

One question at a time on five topics. Get 3 right in a row to master a topic and move on. A miss resets the streak, so you keep practicing a topic until it clicks, up to 20 questions each. Your progress is saved on this device.

How to type free-response answers
One number
Type the number: −5 6.5 7/2. Leave fractions as fractions instead of rounding. Writing x = in front is optional.
Several solutions
Separate them with commas, in any order: 3, −7. Leave out extraneous solutions.
Intervals
Use [ ] when the endpoint is included and ( ) when it isn't. Always use ( ) next to ∞: [3, ∞) (−4, 1). Type inf or tap the ∞ button.
Equations
Use | for absolute value bars and ^ for exponents: y = −4|x + 1| + 6 y = 3(x − 1)^2 + 2. Any equivalent form counts.
